How old were you when you started learning how to work with data?

We might be biased, but we think it’s never too early to start developing data skills!

[This week’s episode](https://community.alteryx.com/t5/Alter-Everything-Podcast/71-Alteryx-your-curriculum/ba-p/642132) of the Alter Everything podcast showcases the prodigious talents of young data explorers Felipe Rodriguez and Ryan Wu, who took on the challenge of Core Certification with Alteryx Designer at the ages of 12 and 16 respectively.

If they’ve inspired you to introduce the young learners in your life to data, check out our Alteryx for K-12 program. This program gives young people the chance to learn about data, gain problem-solving skills and think critically about data. Those abilities open academic and career opportunities — and they’re essential for data literacy in today’s data-driven world. Check out [the full details and sign up](https://www.alteryx.com/why-alteryx/alteryx-for-good/k-12-program-registration?UTM_Content=community).

If you’re not sure where to find the best learning tools, we’ve got a new downloadable guide for you!
