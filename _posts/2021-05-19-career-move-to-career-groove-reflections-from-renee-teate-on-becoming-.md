Renee Teate shared her career move into data science publicly through social media and her podcast, Becoming a Data Scientist. Today, Renee — now director of data science at HelioCampus — joins us for a special episode of our Data Science Mixer podcast to share her experience and advice for others who want to deepen their data science knowledge and advance their careers. Portions of this episode originally premiered at the 2021 Alteryx Inspire conference, and this audio version is the extended cut.
