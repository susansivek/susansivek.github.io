Data science is one of those topics where there's always more to learn. Alteryx is launching a new podcast specifically to help you expand your data science knowledge and practice ... *without* exhausting your brain.

Featuring interviews with key innovators in data science, this podcast is best enjoyed with some top shelf beverages and snacks so you can join our host, data science journalist Susan Currie Sivek, in the happy hour conversation.

Subscribe to  Data Science Mixer on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Stitcher, and on the Alteryx Community so you don’t miss any of the fun. Be sure to listen to the end for bonus resources, and join our Cocktail Conversation by sharing your thoughts on the Alteryx Community or on social media by tagging @Alteryx and #DataScienceMix.

We can’t wait to have you tune in. Cheers!
