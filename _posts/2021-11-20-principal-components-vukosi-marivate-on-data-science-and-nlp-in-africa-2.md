## *Vukosi Marivate, Ph.D., chair of data science at the University of Pretoria, talks about the advancement of data science practice and education in Africa, especially around low-resource languages and NLP.*

· 4 min read

![](https://miro.medium.com/max/10000/0*T2YbiiQXYKYXlX4y)

Photo by on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com?utm_source=medium&utm_medium=referral)

Vukosi Marivate, Ph.D., chair of data science at the University of Pretoria, brought his insights and experience to an episode of the Data Science Mixer podcast for a conversation about the status and growth of data science in Africa, as well as some fascinating collaborations around building natural language processing approaches for low-resource languages. He tells us about the [Masakhane](https://www.masakhane.io/) collaboration and the development of its open-source Python projects, and addresses how the lack of NLP efforts for low-resource languages is not just a data problem, but also a societal problem.

Here are three “principal components” of what Vukosi shared.
