Ben Moss ([@BenMoss](https://community.alteryx.com/t5/user/viewprofilepage/user-id/5143)) is an Alteryx ACE who’s shared some really creative uses of data analytics here on the Data Science Blog. His amazing [Legolytics project](https://community.alteryx.com/t5/Data-Science/Legolytics-Part-1-Extracting-Colours-From-an-Image-With-Alteryx/ba-p/552222) shows how he used Designer to transform a photo of fellow ACE [@PhilipMannering](https://community.alteryx.com/t5/user/viewprofilepage/user-id/14780) into an optimized portrait of Lego blocks, with a shopping list of components ready to buy and build. Another of Ben’s projects, created with [@GwilymTIL](https://community.alteryx.com/t5/user/viewprofilepage/user-id/112603), used the K-nearest neighbors tools in Alteryx to analyze and build [Spotify playlists](https://community.alteryx.com/t5/Data-Science/Alteryx-Your-Discover-Weekly-A-Practical-Application-of-K/ba-p/563359).

After seeing these projects, I wanted to talk with Ben about how these creative projects came about, and what he sees as the role of creativity in data science and analytics. He graciously agreed to a video interview. He also discusses how creative approaches help him and others learn complex topics in fun ways.
